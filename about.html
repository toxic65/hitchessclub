About Us

Who we are and what we stand for.

Our club

The HIT Chess Club was started by students who wanted a place on campus to play seriously and have fun doing it. We welcome every programme and every level, from people learning how the pieces move to rated tournament players.

Vision

A campus where every student can enjoy chess and sharpen their thinking through it.

Mission

To run regular games, coaching and competitions that are open to all HIT students.

Objectives

  • Teach chess to new players
  • Hold at least one tournament each term
  • Represent HIT at inter-university events
  • Build problem-solving and focus
